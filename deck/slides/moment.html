Who it's for

Ananya, three weeks into a new neighbourhood

She has a few dishes she reorders from her old area, a handful of friends who live nearby, and no idea which of the 40 places on her screen is any good.

What she wants

A short list she can trust, with a reason she can check.

What she doesn't want

Ads dressed as advice, or strangers seeing what she orders.

The job to be done

Get a good dinner tonight without researching it.

Ananya is an invented persona for the prototype. "40 places" is illustrative.
