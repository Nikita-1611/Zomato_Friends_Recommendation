Edge cases

What happens when the signal is thin

Quiet area, fewer than 20 people

The group row hides and picks fall back to well-rated dishes, labelled as such.

New user, no orders yet

Three quick taps (veg or non-veg, spice, budget) seed the first picks.

Pick is out of range

A delivery range filter (home or office) drops places that can't reach you.

Growing the friend signal

On the 3rd order of the same dish, we ask once if you'd recommend it to friends.

Each of these is a demo control in the prototype: Simulate quiet area, Simulate new user, Simulate 3rd reorder.
